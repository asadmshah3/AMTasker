# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AMTasker to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/asadmshah3). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors
temporarily or permanently for repeated or serious violations.
